Privacy Policy — Net Tools

Last updated: November 24, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how the Net Tools mobile application (package ID net.tools.sys, the “App”) handles information. The App helps you monitor server availability (ping, port checks, HTTP/HTTPS, and similar) for hosts you configure yourself.

We follow Google Play’s Developer Program Policies, including requirements for disclosing data collection and use.

Summary

  • No account is required.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or analytics.
  • The App has no ads and no developer-operated analytics SDKs.
  • Server lists, monitoring history, and settings are stored on your device.
  • Network access is used only to check hosts you configure. Check results are not sent to the developer’s servers.

2. What data is processed

2.1. Data you enter

The App stores the following locally on your device:

  • Server display names;
  • IP addresses, hostnames, and port/protocol settings (TCP, HTTP, HTTPS, SSH, etc.);
  • List order and other monitoring options you set in the UI.

This information is provided by you. The developer does not receive it over the internet.

2.2. Monitoring data

The device may store:

  • Check outcomes (online/offline, response time, and related technical details);
  • Availability history and statistics while you use monitoring features;
  • Internal state from recent checks.

This is used only to show information in the App and for local notifications.

2.3. App settings

Stored locally, including theme (light/dark), UI language, and scheduled monitoring or notification options if enabled.

2.4. What we do not collect on purpose

We do not collect name, address, phone, payment cards, precise GPS location, or advertising IDs. The App does not require sign-in with Google, Facebook, or other providers.

3. How data is used

On-device data is used only to run checks, display status and history, save preferences, and show local notifications if permitted. We do not use your data for profiling or targeted advertising.

4. Network and third parties

The App connects only to hosts and ports you specify. Server operators may see technical metadata (e.g. your public IP). The developer does not receive check results on developer servers.

HTTP checks (without TLS) may use cleartext traffic for addresses you configure. We do not send data to ad or analytics SDKs. Google Play and Android backup, if enabled, are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Storage, backup, and export

Data is stored on your device. JSON export/import is initiated by you; the developer does not receive exported files. Android backup may include app data per your OS settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Remove servers or history in the App, or uninstall the App. For questions, email the privacy contact above.

8. Security

Lists and history are not sent to the developer. You are responsible for device and network security. HTTPS checks to your servers use TLS to the target host.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s data on developer servers.

10. International users

On-device processing is the same worldwide. You may have privacy rights in your region; contact us using the email in section 1.
